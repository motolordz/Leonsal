Grown-up testing notes

This is an early child-led sensory prototype, not therapy or a diagnostic tool. Sensory preferences differ between children and can also change from day to day.

  • Sound and vibration begin off. No sound is played before a deliberate choice.
  • Use “Gentle motion” to pause decorative animation immediately.
  • Watch for avoidance, covering ears, looking away, distress, repetitive tapping that appears uncomfortable, or difficulty leaving an activity.
  • Let Leon stop, repeat or switch activities without losing a score. Note which controls he understands without prompting.
