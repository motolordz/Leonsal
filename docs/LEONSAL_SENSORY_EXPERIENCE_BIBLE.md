# LeonSal Sensory Experience Bible

This is the adopted Part C sensory-experience specification for LeonSal V2. It is subordinate to the root `AGENTS.md`; stronger child-safety, accessibility, factual teaching, and production-asset rules always win.

## Status

The sensory catalogue in `data/sensory-concepts-v2.json` contains fifty-six concepts. They are a catalogue, not fifty-six completed games. Aliases, themes, saved worlds, and mixer presets do not increase product or game counts.

## Global Sensory Truth

LeonSal uses one shared preference system. Canonical concepts are Motion, Sound, Particles, Speed, Vibration, Calm Mode, and Reduced Motion. Experiences can request gentler settings, but they cannot exceed the current global preference ceiling.

Do not infer autism subtype, emotional state, medical state, dysregulation, sensory seeking, or sensory avoiding from behaviour. The child chooses experiences and preferences.

## No-Pressure Contract

Pure sensory activities default to no-pressure interaction. They must not require score, timer, lives, streak, punishment, loud failure sound, or forced completion.

Every sensory experience must provide a clear exit. Core controls are Home/Back, Settings, Pause where relevant, and Finished. Stop or Finished must never depend on completing a task.

## Runtime Boundaries

Runtime must not load `source-safe-keeping`, rejected assets, QA sheets, contact sheets, or pending/review character art. If approved character art is missing, sensory experiences must use clean procedural/fallback visuals without substituting another character.
